📱 How to Add to iPhone

  1. Click "Save to Photos" to download the pass image
  2. Open Photos app on your iPhone
  3. Find the downloaded pass and add it to your favorites
  4. Quick access: Add to Lock Screen widget for instant access
  5. Scan at campus: Open the photo when you need to scan
⚠️ Note about Apple Wallet: True Apple Wallet integration requires an Apple Developer account ($99/year) and code signing certificates. This generates a visual pass that you can save to your phone's Photos app and use just like a real Wallet pass by showing it when needed.
